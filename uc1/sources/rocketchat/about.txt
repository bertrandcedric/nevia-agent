The data of a Rocket.Chat 8.7 server (users, rooms, subscriptions, messages with threads,
reactions, attachments, settings and Rocket.Chat's internal collections), in MongoDB 8.0 (replica
set rs0) on this computer. Rocket.Chat is stopped and MongoDB's TTL expiry is off from analyse to
the end of the migration: the data does not change (instances and rocketchat_notification_queue
have TTL indexes, so their counts would drift otherwise).
